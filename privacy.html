Privacy Policy

LCK Addict Uploader · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Summary

LCK Addict Uploader is a desktop tool that runs only on its user's own computer. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party data sharing. It does not sell or share any data.

What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (display name and avatar, scope user.info.basic), shown in the app so you can see which account you are posting to.
  • Access and refresh tokens from TikTok Login Kit, used only to upload the videos you choose to your account (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • Creator settings returned by TikTok (such as available privacy options), used to show you the posting options before you confirm.

How it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored in a file on your own computer and are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's own API. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok (and to YouTube, if you choose to post there too), and only when you confirm an upload.

Deleting your data

Disconnect TikTok in the app to delete the stored tokens, or remove the app's access in your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). Videos you posted stay on TikTok until you delete them there.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
