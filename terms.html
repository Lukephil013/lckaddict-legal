LCK Addict Uploader · Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. What the app is

LCK Addict Uploader is a desktop video tool used by the creator of the LCK Addict channel. It edits League of Legends highlight videos, adds English subtitles, and lets the creator publish the finished videos to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It runs on the creator's own computer. It is not a public service and has no user accounts of its own.

2. Using TikTok through the app

  • You sign in with TikTok through TikTok's official Login Kit and choose which permissions to grant.
  • Nothing is posted automatically. Before each upload the app shows a preview of the video and the caption, the privacy setting and interaction settings to choose, and the video is sent to TikTok only after you click to confirm.
  • You are responsible for the content you post and for following the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including having the rights to any footage you use.
  • You can disconnect TikTok at any time in the app, or by removing the app's access in your TikTok settings.
